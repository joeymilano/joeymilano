### [Finfold](https://github.com/joeymilano/Finfold) · AI marketing agent

I built Finfold for small teams that need to turn one product update into channel-specific drafts. It surfaces opportunities, remembers brand rules, and gives people a review step before anything goes live.

一个产品更新，可以变成适合小红书、微信公众号和 LinkedIn 各自语境的草稿。发什么、什么时候发，由人决定。

[Try Finfold](https://www.finfold.app/) · [Design case study](https://joeyzhao.cc/en/finfold) · [Growth Mission API & MCP](https://github.com/joeymilano/finfold-growth-mission-api)

### [MissionGet](https://joeyzhao.cc/en/missionget-glasses) · AI glasses for office work

I worked with the MissionGet team on the product definition, interaction design, and prototype integration. The prototype lets you start a request by voice, review it on a phone, and continue in the desktop workbench.

戴着眼镜说出一个任务，在手机上确认，回到电脑继续做。这个原型关注的是三台设备之间怎么接得上。

[Try the interactive demo](https://joeyzhao.cc/en/missionget-glasses#try)

### [魔都人生 · Shanghai Stories](https://github.com/joeymilano/modu-life) · Life sim

A browser game about arriving in Shanghai at eighteen. You start with five cards and a series of choices about work, rent, and the people you meet.

十八岁到上海，手里五张牌。先找工作、先交房租，还是跟刚认识的人出去走走？

[Play in your browser](https://modulife.pages.dev/) · [Browse the source](https://github.com/joeymilano/modu-life)
